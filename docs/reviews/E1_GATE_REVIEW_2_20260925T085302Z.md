## High-severity findings

1. **The inlet-by-phase momentum rule is undefined for absent phases.**
   Gate line 222 says “for each inlet patch and phase” and divides by
   `norm(M_ref,q,p)`. On a pure-water inlet the air reference is the zero
   vector; on the pure-air inlet the water reference is the zero vector.
   Neither relative error nor a relative component tolerance exists there.
   Gate lines 239–240 and 477–479 and audit line 36 repeat the requirement;
   audit's claim that it avoids division by zero is only true for the two
   *present-phase* pairs. The active-pair formula and signs themselves are
   correct: water/nozzle has negative y momentum and zero x/z; air/gas-inlet
   has positive x momentum and zero y/z. **Minimum fix:** explicitly enumerate
   these two nonzero-reference checks, then define separate absent-phase
   leakage checks with prospectively chosen nonzero scales or absolute units.
   For an absent phase all three prescribed components are zero. Record a
   structured absent-reference status rather than evaluating `0/0`, silently
   passing NaN, or normalizing by another inlet's momentum. Apply the same
   zero-denominator discipline to any source-normalized check at `t=0`.

2. **The “complete proposal” still lacks important initial and numerical
   choices.** Gate lines 93–100 identify unreported initial velocity, but the
   matrix at lines 130–150 only selects initial turbulence: it never chooses
   internal `U`, initial liquid distribution/inventory, initial static
   pressure/`p_rgh`, or whether the air field is initially uniform, at rest or
   pre-developed. Listing “initialization” at lines 317–318 does not make that
   choice. The short final window cannot erase an unspecified startup by
   assertion. Line 148 gives momentum convection and PIMPLE counts but not
   k/epsilon convection, linear-solver tolerances/termination, or inlet/wall
   pressure conditions. The PDF, p. 6 / article p. 5, also identifies SIMPLE
   and second-order convection for both momentum and turbulence; the proposed
   PIMPLE coupling is an additional named deviation. **Minimum fix:** append
   explicit provisional initial-field and remaining boundary/scheme choices,
   or enumerate them as unresolved inputs with an owner and a required
   before-run decision. Preserve the distinction between a reviewable
   proposal and the later complete, hashed dictionaries. Do not claim the
   current matrix already freezes all physics/numerics.

3. **The capability audit still permits choosing wall geometry after comparing
   results.** Audit line 21 says to compare flat and curved cases “before
   choosing the main reconstruction.” Gate lines 107–108, 142 and 145 instead
   prescribe a prospective curved primary and separately reported sensitivities.
   This conflict reintroduces the selection bias identified in the initial
   review. **Minimum fix:** remove result-dependent primary selection from the
   audit; freeze the curved trace and primary model prospectively. Report
   flat-wall and turbulence variants as separately named sensitivities under
   a declared matrix, without selecting the best Figure 13 agreement. Any
   replacement primary needs a prospective amendment and retained attempts.

## Medium-severity findings

4. **Preparation, static checks and flow-dependent evidence need a feasible
   order.** Gate lines 147 and 266–272 require wall-y+ evidence/deviation review
   before solver execution; lines 149 and 373 and audit lines 32/58 require
   actual candidate Courant behavior before execution/timestep freezing.
   `checkMesh` supplies geometry, not a developed wall shear or the evolving
   near-interface fluxes. Initial-field Co is not a bound on later Co, and an
   all-air initial field can have no interface at all. The separately mentioned
   characterization pilot at gate line 372 needs an explicit place in this
   sequence. In addition, gate lines 152–159, 388–394 and 446–458 and audit
   lines 3/29 impose fresh approval even for draft geometry/dictionary
   preparation already authorized by the user. **Minimum fix:** recognize that
   existing preparation authorization; keep primary resource scheduling and
   scientific acceptance separate. Require static mesh/input checks before a
   separately preregistered, independently reviewed characterization run;
   collect y+, evolving global/interface Co, closure and costs during that
   bounded run; then review evidence and freeze the comparison mesh/dt pair.
   Apply runtime stops on every later run. Do not require unavailable future
   flow evidence to create draft inputs, or call scale estimates measurements.
   This review grants no characterization or meshing allocation.

5. **The finite temporal sample is described as covering every time in an
   interval.** Gate line 150 calls the entire `[4.50,5.00] s` interval the
   timing-uncertainty set, while lines 195–199 define only 51 discrete profiles.
   Lines 228–230 then imply that no unreported Figure 13 time inside the
   interval escapes the check. Sampling every 10 ms does not bound intervening
   extrema or threshold-contour changes. **Minimum fix:** explicitly restrict
   the claim and timing set to the 51 sampled solver states, retaining unknown
   between-sample behavior as a limitation, or preregister an output-cadence
   refinement/dense compact-observable check with a numerical adequacy rule.
   This is distinct from halving the solver timestep. Freeze the exact
   station-plane/alpha interpolation and threshold-crossing procedure with the
   future sampler; otherwise the 51 contour values are not yet reproducible.
   The mean of instantaneous observables, full-span inference, detached-liquid
   policy and separate read bounds are already clearly labelled proposals.

6. **One score and decision rule must replace the remaining contradictory
   prose.** Gate lines 215–216 correctly define an aggregate curve NRMSE over
   fixed stations, but lines 244–245 say NRMSE must hold “at every frozen
   station.” These are different checks: a single residual of `0.4*R` among
   20 otherwise exact points yields curve NRMSE `0.4/sqrt(20)=8.94%`, while its
   pointwise normalized error is 40%. Gate lines 290–291 also assign the
   “whole-window time-shift comparison” a 5% bound, although curve comparison
   is 10% and temporal range/stationarity is 5%. **Minimum fix:** freeze one
   aggregate NRMSE per observable/profile over all required stations, taking
   the maximum upper bound over the declared temporal profiles plus the mean;
   keep per-station temporal range as a separate 5% check. If pointwise error
   limits are wanted, propose them explicitly rather than implying they follow
   from NRMSE. State precedence: invalid attempt, unresolved numerical or
   stationarity gate, source-bound overlap, then pass/curve disagreement.
   A curve disagreement with failed mesh/time refinement is descriptive, not
   an accepted source-comparison failure (compare lines 250–251 and 286–289).

7. **Logged phase boundedness is not yet a validity requirement.** Audit line
   17 requests per-step alpha minima/maxima, but the gate's validity/pass
   conjunction at lines 234–260 does not define a boundedness limit or handling
   of violations. Global water closure alone cannot exclude compensating
   negative and greater-than-one volume fractions. **Minimum fix:** add the
   alpha-boundedness diagnostic, an explicitly proposed numerical tolerance
   and violation disposition to the operational validity record. Separate any
   solver clipping/correction from raw extrema and conserved phase flux. This
   is a before-run contract decision, not evidence that an unrun case failed.

## Confirmed corrections and arithmetic

- Gate line 146 and audit lines 20/31 correctly propose
  `p_rgh = p_gauge - rho*g dot (x-x_ref)` with **local mixture density**.
  At y = -10 m relative to the nozzle and `p_gauge=0`, an air-only face has
  `p_rgh=-115.758 Pa`; a pure-water face would have `-97864.56 Pa`, with
  mixtures interpolated through rho. An air-only constant density cannot be
  substituted when liquid exits. Constant static atmospheric pressure is
  not the same as a hydrostatic static-pressure profile: this is a pressure
  reference/BC proposal. The actual dictionary/reference-height mapping and
  facewise behavior remain unimplemented and untested in an E1 case.
- The inward convective momentum formula has units N and the active-phase
  signs above. Independent arithmetic gives inlet area `0.125663706 m^2`,
  liquid flow `1.256637061 m^3/s`, mass flow `1253.621132 kg/s`, and water
  momentum `(0,-12536.211325,0) N`. It is an inlet flux check, not a complete
  control-volume momentum closure, as gate line 222 now states.
- Recalculation gives `q=17.2535455`, `We_g=32122.2222`,
  `We_j=554222.2222`, `Re_g=1776344.0860`, `Re_j=4488638.9201`.
  Carrying the printed `4.9e6` discrepancy rather than changing viscosity is
  correct. The proposed turbulence conversions give
  `epsilon_j=0.0107810420` and `epsilon_g=3.6978974178 m^2/s^3`.
- The primary CSV contains exactly 20 positive-X penetration and 10 positive-X
  width rows; the specified grids and ranges `R_Y=5.225`, `R_Z=3.257` match.
  Excluding the calibration origin, using positive-downward plotted Y with
  physical +y upward, and retaining full width as an inference correct the
  earlier issues. Figure 13's author timing/extraction details are still not
  recovered. Figure 16's shared-q marker properly remains contextual.
- Courant scale arithmetic is correct. For an interface cell moving at 70 m/s
  with 16 mm aligned spacing, the proposed interface cap 1 would imply
  `dt<=0.228571 ms`, not 1 or 0.5 ms. This is conditional arithmetic, not a
  measured E1 restriction. The draft properly calls both caps unapproved stops
  and distinguishes STAR-CCM+ CFL from OpenFOAM accuracy evidence.
- The isoAdvector/HRIC, wall-model and mesh-family differences are disclosed.
  The CPU image is pinned in the proposed commands, which are explicitly
  unexecuted placeholders. Their 18-CPU/16-rank/48-GiB settings and 24-hour
  timeout are proposals, not resource reservations or E1 cost evidence.
  A future primary-scheduled job still needs measured available headroom,
  a bounded record and a functioning monitor. No image/runtime inspection was
  repeated in this review.
- Gate lines 29–33, 115–119 and 492–498 now correctly keep E2 execution closed
  until an independently accepted E1 disposition expressly permits it. A
  failed, inconclusive or descriptive-only E1 does not silently satisfy that
  dependency. All tolerances remain proposed, not frozen or approved.
