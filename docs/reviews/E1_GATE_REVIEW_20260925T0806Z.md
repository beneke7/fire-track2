## Blocking and high-severity findings

1. **Inputs and numerical limits are not frozen.** Turbulence/wall pairing,
   inlet epsilon and air turbulence, curved-wall geometry/contact angle, actual
   discretization dictionaries, CFL limits, and stationarity criterion remain
   open in the draft. The phrase “reviewer-ready candidate execution contract”
   overstates readiness. Add a concrete provisional input/limit matrix and a
   deviation register. Author clarification is required only to claim exact
   recovery of paper settings.
2. **Outlet pressure mapping is wrong for variable elevation.** The draft's
   zero-gauge `p_rgh` outlet does not impose constant atmospheric static
   pressure because `p_rgh = p_gauge - rho*gh`. Freeze gravity, reference
   height, density and static-pressure conventions; use and verify a
   static-pressure-derived condition such as
   [`prghPressure`](https://doc.openfoam.com/2306/tools/processing/boundary-conditions/rtm/derived/general/prghPressure/).
   Also define reverse-flow velocity, alpha and turbulence behavior. Across
   10 m the air contribution alone is 115.758 Pa. The same mapping issue is in
   `experiments/E1_CPU_SOLVER_CAPABILITY_AUDIT.md`.
3. **Coordinate/origin conventions do not support the proposed score yet.**
   The simulation uses downward `-y`, but the draft defines positive-downward
   `Y=y/d_j`. Define `X=(x-x0)/d_j`, `Y=(y0-y_min)/d_j`, and
   `Z=(z_max-z_min)/d_j`. The scored CSV includes a `(0,0)` width/origin anchor
   which the raster digitizer hardcodes rather than traces. Decide whether to
   keep it as contextual calibration data or justify a physical mapping; freeze
   the scored rows and normalization range prospectively.
4. **Momentum tolerances and overall pass conjunction are incomplete.** The
   draft's componentwise 0.5% criterion divides by prescribed x/z momentum
   components that are zero. Its pass conjunction also omits momentum,
   stationarity and CFL checks. Normalize by the nonzero prescribed vector
   magnitude or define defensible absolute tolerances for zero components, and
   require every mandatory integrity, conservation, numerical and stationarity
   check to pass before curve agreement can pass E1.
5. **E2 wording permits failed or inconclusive E1.** Several clauses require
   only an “independent E1 decision.” Require an **accepted E1 disposition**
   authorizing advancement; failed or descriptive-only E1 keeps E2 execution
   closed unless a separately reviewed prospective scope amendment is approved.
   Independent E2 method preparation can continue.

## Medium-severity findings

6. **Uncertainty and comparison-domain operations are ambiguous.** Define
   piecewise-linear slope bounds across the full time interval, including
   endpoint treatment, and require every frozen scored station to be covered.
   Freeze mean-of-observables versus contour-of-mean and temporal weighting.
7. **Mesh and execution records need completion.** Promote prism-layer settings
   to prepared-mesh acceptance/deviation fields, choose the primary wall variant
   prospectively rather than against Figure 13 agreement, pin the image ID, and
   retain all 51 sampling outputs. The commands are properly labelled
   placeholders. Mesh review can be preparatory and should not be confused with
   final execution approval.
