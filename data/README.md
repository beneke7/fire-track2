# Data provenance

The eight original PDFs remain in the repository root. `make papers` extracts a
local cache into ignored `derived/papers/` and records each original's SHA-256.
PDF page numbers in source notes refer to the PDF's page order unless explicitly
identified as printed journal pages. Text extraction preserves page separators;
verify equations and figures against the original PDF before implementing them.

Future digitized measurements belong in experiment-specific input directories
with the source hash, page/figure/table, axis units, digitization procedure,
uncertainty and any transformations. Preserve raw points alongside processed data.
Separate calibration and validation data. Never infer precise measurements from
a rendered illustration without documenting that limitation.

The current [`derived/rouaix_e1_case1_fig13.csv`](derived/rouaix_e1_case1_fig13.csv)
contains the primary SVG-path trace of Rouaix et al. Figure 13, Case 1. The
[`derived/rouaix_e1_case1_fig13_raster.csv`](derived/rouaix_e1_case1_fig13_raster.csv)
contains a separate pixel-based trace of the same manuscript figure. Their
source locations, calibrations, bounds, and unresolved figure timing/width
interpretation are recorded in
[`E1_ROUAIX_CASE1_SOURCE.md`](../experiments/E1_ROUAIX_CASE1_SOURCE.md). Both
files are digitized figures, not raw experimental data or acceptance targets.
Section 4.3's fits are cross-case correlations and the source does not report
fit scatter, so residuals against the case are a secondary consistency check,
not a demonstrated mismatch or contradiction.
The first trace pass of the Calbrix Dash-8 Fig. 4 scalar maximum-velocity curve
is recorded in
[`derived/calbrix_dash8_fig4_velocity.csv`](derived/calbrix_dash8_fig4_velocity.csv).
A separately calibrated second read is in
[`derived/calbrix_dash8_fig4_velocity_independent.csv`](derived/calbrix_dash8_fig4_velocity_independent.csv),
generated by
[`digitize_calbrix_dash8_fig4_velocity_independent.py`](../scripts/digitize_calbrix_dash8_fig4_velocity_independent.py).
Both are figure-read traces, not raw data; the paper does not publish the exact
discharge history or inlet profile needed for an exact E2 replay. See the E2
source record for calibration, read bounds, comparison and limits.

The [`derived/calbrix_dash8_cloud_curves.csv`](derived/calbrix_dash8_cloud_curves.csv)
contains primary raster traces of the Dash-8 `alpha_L=0.001` cloud-envelope
curves from Figs. 6–9: penetration `Z(y)`, normalized penetration, dimensional
width `L(z)`, and normalized expansion. Its read bounds reflect rendered pixel
and stroke width; it is a figure read, not raw data. A separately calibrated
second read is in
[`derived/calbrix_dash8_cloud_curves_independent.csv`](derived/calbrix_dash8_cloud_curves_independent.csv).
The two figure reads agree within summed raster-read bounds for 94.1–100% of
primary samples across the compared series. Small disconnected Fig. 9 red/blue
marks near the origin are retained as separate, uncertain segments. The Fig. 8
time conflict remains unresolved, and normalized curves cannot be converted to
dimensional coordinates without the unreported exit area `S`. See the E2
source record for trace calibration, source hash, comparison method and limits.

The E3 CL-415 source record is in
[`E3_CALBRIX_CL415_SOURCE.md`](../experiments/E3_CALBRIX_CL415_SOURCE.md).
It documents the four exits shown in the paper, source-history and figure-label
ambiguities, and missing outlet/flow inputs. First raster reads of the red/top
and green/bottom scalar maximum-velocity curves in Fig. 4 are in
[`derived/calbrix_cl415_fig4_velocities.csv`](derived/calbrix_cl415_fig4_velocities.csv),
generated by [`digitize_calbrix_e3_fig4.py`](../scripts/digitize_calbrix_e3_fig4.py).
The tails remain positive and do not establish a physical shutoff; these are
not four per-port histories or discharge flow rates. The independent second
trace agrees at shared sample times within combined raster and time-read
allowances, while the visible tails end at different times. Neither trace
establishes physical shutoff; no zero is appended. See the E3 source record for
comparison details.

The identified-liquid-structure counts in Fig. 11(a), not parcel counts, have
two raster reads:
[`derived/calbrix_cl415_fig11_structure_counts.csv`](derived/calbrix_cl415_fig11_structure_counts.csv)
and
[`derived/calbrix_cl415_fig11_structure_counts_independent.csv`](derived/calbrix_cl415_fig11_structure_counts_independent.csv).
Their independent pixel-read audit is in
[`E3_FIG11_INDEPENDENT_TRACE_AUDIT.md`](../experiments/E3_FIG11_INDEPENDENT_TRACE_AUDIT.md).
The traces agree within their figure-read bounds, but the paper's MATLAB
structure-detection method is unpublished, so Fig. 11 remains descriptive and
does not provide a quantitative acceptance target or make E3 run-ready.

The M134 Marana trial's Fig. 9 plotted-marker trace is in
[`derived/amorim_m134_fig9.csv`](derived/amorim_m134_fig9.csv), generated by
[`digitize_amorim_m134_fig9.py`](../scripts/digitize_amorim_m134_fig9.py).
It records measured and ADM marker centers from the vector PDF, not raw
cup-level data. The paper's `Vx` is a cross-track sum at each along-track
position; the figure's x origin was adjusted and must not be treated as an
absolute aircraft position. See
[`E4_AMORIM_M134_SOURCE.md`](../experiments/E4_AMORIM_M134_SOURCE.md) for the
source hash, calibration, interpretation, and limits.
